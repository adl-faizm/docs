## What's included

XLSMART Design System covers every layer of your UI stack:

- **Design Tokens** **(Foundations)** — a single source of truth for colors, typography, spacing, elevation, and motion
- **UI components** — 20\+ accessible, production-ready components built with consistency in mind
- **Guidelines** — best practices for accessibility, responsive design, iconography, and content
- **Figma library** — auto-synced component library so your designs always match production
- **Theming** — token-based theming that lets you customize the system for any brand or product
