The XLSMART grid system provides a responsive structure for organizing and aligning content across different screen sizes. It helps create consistent layouts while allowing content to adapt based on viewport, hierarchy, and product needs.

## A. Overview

Grid helps to:

- Maintains alignment between elements - Establishes hierarchy and content structure - Supports responsive layouts - Ensures cross-product consistency - Aligns design and development - Controls content width on large screens

## B. Grid Anatomy

Every page layout is built on 3 core elements:

1. **Columns**: divide the content area into proportional sections and provide alignment points for page-level content. 2. **Gutters**: spaces between columns. They separate content while maintaining consistent alignment. 3. **Margin**: spaces between the content grid and the outer edge of the viewport or main content area.

### Mobile

- Full-width content: 4 columns - Two side-by-side items: 2 \+ 2 columns - Avoid 3 \+ 1 column layouts for main content as they tend to be too narrow

## C. Responsive Breakpoints & Grid Matrix

Breakpoints represent points where the layout needs to adapt, not specific device models.

| Breakpoint | Viewport | Columens | Gutter | Margin | Max Content Width |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mobile | 320px – 767px | 4 | 16px | 16px | 100% |
| Tablet | 768px – 1023px | 8 | 24px | 24px | 100% |
| Desktop | 1024px – 1439px | 12 | 24px | 64px | 1024px |
| Large Desktop | 1440px\+ | 12 | 24px | Auto | 1200px |

## D. Layout Behaviour Models

### 1. Centered Layout (Default for Web Portal & Marketing)

- **Concept**: Used for public websites, onboarding pages, e-commerce, and article-based content. - **Rule**: Main content width is capped at **Max-Width: 1200px**. When the screen expands beyond 1440px, the content stays symmetrically centered. - **UX Value**: Prevents text lines and cards from stretching too wide on ultra-wide monitors, reducing eye strain.

### 2. Full-Bleed / Fluid Layout (For Dashboards & Complex SaaS Apps)

- **Concept**: Reserved for internal tools, analytics dashboards, and data-dense applications (e.g., data tables). - **Rule**: Content expands to fill 100% of the screen width while locking the **Outer Margin** at **32px** or **48px**. Grid columns stretch dynamically to adapt to the user's monitor resolution.
